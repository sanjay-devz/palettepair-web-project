01 / ABOUT LUA

What is LUA?

LUA is a creative tool designed to help UI/UX designers discover beautiful color combinations and font pairings. It brings color and typography together to make the design process simpler and more inspiring.

02 / WHY WE’RE HERE

Our purpose

We believe great design is not just about choosing colors. It’s about creating balance, readability, and visual harmony.
